Reading this overlay
These layers are independent. Each quality is a separate assessment with its own inputs, assumptions, spatial scale and resolution — they are not coupled, and they do not share a common calibration. Overlapping them lets you compare where qualities balance or conflict across the area, but agreement between two layers is a prompt to look closer, not a confirmation.
